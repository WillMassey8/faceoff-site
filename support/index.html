Support

Need help? Email support email coming soon. We usually reply within 2 business days. Include your fighter name and your device model.

How does a match work?

You and your rival see the same players in the same order. Type the name and tap Lock in. Right answers knock health off your rival's bar, and faster answers hit harder. Empty their bar or lead after the last round to win.

I typed it right but it said Missed!

Small typos and last names usually count. Accents don't matter. If you think we got one wrong, email us the player and round, and we'll fix it.

Who am I playing?

A real player. Your rival's run was recorded earlier on the same players, with their real answers and timing, so there's always someone to play. Your runs are recorded too and other players face them. When your fighter wins one of those, you get a notification and 5 coins.

What is the Practice Bot?

It's only in the 3-round practice duel during onboarding, and it's always labeled as a bot.

How do I earn coins, gems and packs?

Win matches, land correct answers and combos, finish daily quests, follow the Road and keep your streak. Coins are spent in the Locker and Shop on mascot drip. Gems are spent in the Daily Gem Shop and can also open packs.

Can I buy coins, gems or packs?

Not in this version. Coins, gems and packs are earned by playing. Packs (Rookie, Pro, All-Star and Hall of Fame) give five pulls of drip, gems and more. Odds are always shown before you open one. Nothing costs real money.

How do I change my mascot or outfit?

Open the Locker tab.

How do I turn notifications on or off?

iOS Settings → Face Off → Notifications.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Settings (gear) → Delete account → confirm. It's permanent and removes your profile, items and history.

Where do the photos come from?

Wikimedia Commons, under public domain or Creative Commons licenses. Each photo shows its credit. A full list is on the credits page. If you're a photographer or rights holder and want a photo changed, email us.

Report or block a player, handle or crew

In the app, report or block a handle or a crew from their profile. You can also email support email coming soon with the handle or crew name and what happened.
