Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Face Off ("we", "us") makes the Face Off: 1v1 Sports Trivia app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

Account info. If you sign in with Apple, we receive a user ID and, if you choose to share it, your email (Apple may give us a private relay address). If you sign in with email, we store your email address. We never see your Apple ID password.

Game profile. Your fighter name (handle), chosen mascot and outfit, favorite sport, coins, gems, packs, rank, league, streaks, quests, achievements, collection album and match history (opponents, answers, scores, timing).

Device and diagnostic info. Basic technical data needed to run the service, such as app version, device type, and crash or error logs. If you allow notifications, we store a push token so we can send match invites and reminders.

What we do NOT collect. No precise location, no contacts, no photos from your device, no health data, no advertising ID. We do not sell your data and we do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

How we use it

  • To run matches, match you with opponents, score answers and award rewards. Your recorded match runs (handle, mascot and outfit, answers and timing) are replayed as an opponent for other players, and you're notified of the results.
  • To show your handle, mascot, rank and league position to other players (public game profile).
  • To send notifications you've allowed (you can turn them off anytime in iOS Settings).
  • To keep the game fair and secure (anti-cheat, abuse prevention) and to fix bugs.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers that host our backend and database (Lovable Cloud / Supabase) and deliver push notifications (Apple Push Notification service). They process data only on our behalf.
  • Other players see your public game profile only: handle, mascot, rank, league and match results.
  • Legal reasons when required by law.

Photos in the game

Athlete photos come from Wikimedia Commons under public domain or Creative Commons licenses and are credited in the app and on our credits page. Face Off is not affiliated with any league, team or player.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, inventory, wallet and match history within 30 days (backups roll off within 30 more days). If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke the Sign in with Apple token.

Children

Face Off is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to processing. Email support email coming soon and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected with access controls. Game results and rewards are calculated on our servers.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for big changes, tell you in the app.
